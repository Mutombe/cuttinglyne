Our services

Six services that were each added because a client asked us to stop handing their cargo to someone else — and none of them are subcontracted.

01 — Sea Freight

Sea Freight

FCL, LCL, breakbulk and reefer on weekly allocations with the lines that actually call your port.

  • FCL and LCL on fixed weekly allocation
  • Reefer and temperature-controlled equipment
  • Breakbulk and flat-rack for out-of-gauge
  • Origin consolidation at four Asian hubs

02 — Air Freight

Air Freight

Next-flight-out, back-to-back consolidations and full charter when the ship date has already gone.

  • Next-flight-out for AOG and critical spares
  • Twice-weekly consolidations ex Europe and Asia
  • Charter broking for oversized and urgent lifts
  • Dangerous goods handled by IATA-certified staff

03 — Road Haulage

Road Haulage

Cross-border and domestic linehaul on tracked fleets, including sidelifters and abnormal loads.

  • SADC cross-border with own clearing agents
  • Sidelifter and tautliner fleet
  • Abnormal load permits and pilot vehicles
  • Live GPS with geofenced milestone alerts

Warehousing

Bonded and general storage with pick-pack, labelling, kitting and returns under a single WMS.

  • Cycle counting with client portal access
  • Returns processing and refurbishment

Customs Clearance

Tariff rulings, duty optimisation, refund claims and audit-ready records filed by our own brokers.
