How a booking runs

From cut-off
to delivery.

The same four beats on every ocean booking, with a named coordinator holding the file throughout.

Book & confirm

Allocation secured against your forecast, with the stack dates and cut-off confirmed in writing.

Collect & stuff

Collection scheduled, container stuffed and a packing list matched to the commercial invoice.

Sail & pre-clear

Entry filed while the vessel is on the water so release lands with arrival, not after it.

Deliver & cost

Final mile booked off the release, then POD and final costing sent together.
