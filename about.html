Our story

How we got here.

No acquisitions, no rebrands. Just lanes added one at a time as clients asked us to go further.

2008 — Two desks

Opened as a customs brokerage clearing reefer cargo through Durban for three fruit exporters.

2013 — First bond store

Took over 4,000 m² of bonded space so clients could defer duty instead of financing it.

2018 — Air and road

Added air consolidations and a cross-border fleet after one too many missed sailings.

Today — 40 ports

Eleven offices, four bonded sites and our own people in every corridor we sell.
